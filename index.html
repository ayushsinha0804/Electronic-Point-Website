Who We Are

Electronic Point has been repairing and supplying industrial electronics from Adityapur, Jamshedpur since 2008. In that time we have become the name the region turns to for one specific problem that every factory in Jharkhand's industrial belt eventually faces: a critical drive, controller or power electronics unit has failed, production has stopped, and the OEM is quoting weeks and lakhs to replace it.

We repair it instead. At the component level. In days rather than weeks, at a fraction of replacement cost. That is not a marketing line — it is what our billing history shows, drive after drive, plant after plant, since the day we opened.

Most industrial electronics "repair" is replacement. We work differently: we open equipment to the component level, find what actually failed, and replace exactly that — saving clients an average of 60 to 80 per cent versus OEM replacement cost. That's why Tata Steel, Ramkrishna Forgings, Brakes India, Steel Strips Wheels and more than a hundred other plants trust us with their most critical equipment.
